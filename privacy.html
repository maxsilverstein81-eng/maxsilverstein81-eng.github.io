Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-27.

Ultron is a personal app used only by its owner. With the owner's permission it reads the owner's Google data (Gmail metadata, drafts, calendar, health data) on the owner's own computer to help the owner plan their day.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Google user data is not used to train AI models. Data stays on the owner's computer except where the owner's own services process it.

Ultron's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To revoke access, remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
